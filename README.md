Customer Behaviour and  Churn Analysis

AnalystLab Africa - Data Analytics Internship | Week 1
This repository contains my Week 1 Data Analytics Internship project completed as part of the AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship Programme.
The project focuses on Customer Behaviour and Churn Analysis for ABC Communications Ltd, with the objective of understanding customer churn patterns, identifying high-risk customer segments, and developing data-driven recommendations to support customer retention.
Business Problem
ABC Communications Ltd is seeking to better understand customer churn and identify opportunities to improve customer retention.
The analysis investigates customer characteristics, contract types, tenure, services, payment methods, and other customer attributes to identify patterns associated with churn.
Business Questions
The analysis addresses the following questions:
What does the customer base look like?
Which customer segments have the highest churn?
Does contract type influence retention?
Does tenure affect customer loyalty?
Which services are associated with churn?
Which payment methods have higher churn?
What actions should management take?
